# Shared metric collision shell for the closed Rocket Arena

Task 6.1 introduces an immutable shared collision descriptor, resolves the metric arena into 34 mirrored primitives, and projects that table directly into Rapier with semantic surface metadata. The canonical dimensions, centered openings, solid goal interiors, capability tags, descriptor projection, and construction rollback pass the focused suites. **High — confirmed:** the lower end ramps are not capped where they meet the goal jambs, leaving point-sized paths into an under-ramp cavity and beyond the goal line. **Medium — confirmed:** the 60 m/s harness checks ball centers against an expanded coarse envelope, so it can pass positions beyond the exterior shell while never exercising the ramps, upper transitions, or their seams.

Watch for: **High — confirmed:** open lower goal-jamb ramp seams violate the closed-volume contract; **Medium — confirmed:** the containment test overclaims whole-ball and full-boundary coverage.

**Verdict**: NEEDS_CHANGES

## High-level view

The shared resolver makes the previously implicit 0.5 m shell, 2.56 m-by-2.56 m lower ramps, 2.56 m-by-2.56 m upper transitions, and 11.52 m axis-retreat corner cuts deterministic. Mirrored loops produce both ends and all corners, and the server maintains no second geometry model.

**High — confirmed:** topology is open at the lower goal jambs. Each end ramp is a thin sloped cuboid ending at `x = ±8.93`, while the goal side wall starts only at `z = ±51.2`; no triangular cap closes the space below the ramp, and that space opens past the end plane outside the goal.

**Medium — confirmed:** the runtime containment proof represents neither the ball volume nor the sloped boundary planes. It permits the center through the shell thickness plus 0.35 m and samples vertical walls, one corner, floor, ceiling, and goal backs, leaving ramps, upper transitions, opening edges, and seam-directed trajectories untested.

Collider creation is transactional: reverse-order cleanup removes already-created colliders if construction fails. Fault injection on the fifth `createCollider` call left zero colliders, and the checked-in success, setup-failure, and assertion-failure paths free every tracked world.

The deleted `createGoalSensors` and `checkGoal` exports have no live in-repository imports, so Task 6.3's swept center-plane scorer remains untouched. Task 6.1 and Task 6.2 are independently checkable because visible-boundary alignment belongs to Requirement 12.10; Task 6.1 itself cannot be checked until the two findings are resolved.

## Lower end ramps do not close against the goal jambs

**High — confirmed.** The end-ramp pieces span from each opening edge to its corner at `shared/src/geometry/arena-collision.ts:250-268`, and the goal side walls begin at the goal line at `shared/src/geometry/arena-collision.ts:390-406`. Because each ramp is only a thin cuboid, its lateral face covers the slab thickness rather than the triangular region bounded by the floor, 45-degree ramp, and goal line. No primitive fills that region.

At either goal, a Rapier ray at `y = 1` and `|z| = 50.5` crosses both `x = -8.93` and `x = 8.93` without hitting fixed geometry. From inside that cavity, rays at `|x| = 10` continue outward past `|z| = 51.2` without a hit; all four mirrored probes returned `null`.

```
At |x| just outside the opening, in the y/z plane:

y
2.56                 end wall starts here
  |                /|
  |              /  |
  |   thin ramp /    |     unfilled region below ramp
0 +____________/.....+--------------------> z
             48.64  51.2 (goal line)       exterior
```

The 0.9125 m ball may be too large to traverse the narrowest point without penetration, but Requirement 12.8 requires a closed collision volume rather than gaps smaller than one body. Add mirrored profile-shaped caps or solid ramp wedges at all four goal jambs, audit every lateral ramp termination, and test point rays plus swept bodies across every seam.

## The 60 m/s harness admits escaped centers and omits transition impacts

**Medium — confirmed.** `isInsideClosedVolume` at `server/src/physics/test-metric-arena.ts:136-158` validates only the body center and expands each nominal limit by the 0.5 m shell plus `CONTAINMENT_TOLERANCE = 0.35` from line 20. Whole-ball containment at the east wall requires the 0.9125 m-radius center at or inside `x = 40.0475`, but the assertion passes through `x = 41.81`, 0.35 m beyond the collider's exterior face. The predicate also classifies the air below every ramp as inside because it has no ramp-plane distance check.

The matrix at `server/src/physics/test-metric-arena.ts:202-216` targets two side walls, two vertical end-wall portions, one corner, floor, ceiling, and both goal backs. It never targets a lower ramp, upper transition, opening edge, lateral cap, or floor/ramp/wall/corner/ceiling seam, so tunneling into an under-ramp cavity does not necessarily fail the oracle.

Use sphere-aware signed distances from every interior surface, shrinking boundaries by the authoritative radius and allowing only a numerical penetration tolerance. Add mirrored 60 m/s trajectories for every lower ramp and upper transition, every floor/ramp/wall/corner/ceiling join, opening jamb/top, and all goal floor/side/roof/back seams.
