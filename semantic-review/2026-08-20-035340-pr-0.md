# Shared metric arena boundary rendering

The change adds a shared planar-boundary resolver and rebuilds the client field, perimeter, corner cuts, ceiling, and goal tunnels from `ArenaGeometrySpec`. The corner endpoints and zero-thickness goal tunnel faces are internally consistent with the metric dimensions, and the resolver correctly admits that the spec cannot determine the ramp or wall-ceiling profiles. It is not ready to ship: the active collision world still uses legacy dimensions, the renderer substitutes sharp seams for eight unresolved surfaces, and visible trim exceeds the 0.05 m tolerance. The passing tests cannot detect those failures because their oracle and mesh selection come from the implementation itself.

**Watch for:** **High · confirmed** — metric rendering runs against the legacy 40 m × 60 m collision shell; **High · confirmed** — full floor, wall, and ceiling patches visually replace unresolved ramp/ceiling profiles; **High · confirmed** — goal frames, ribs, rails, and corner posts intrude 0.055–0.09 m into authoritative space while tests exclude them; **Medium · confirmed** — the alignment assertions compare the renderer with its own resolver and inspect only tagged position-buffer extrema.

**Verdict**: NEEDS_CHANGES

## High-level view

The renderer now consumes one shared patch vocabulary instead of reconstructing most dimensions. That patch set describes an 81.92 m × 102.4 m field, while the active Rapier builder still constructs the 40 m × 60 m legacy shell, leaving visible and physical boundaries far apart.

Marking four floor-wall ramps and four wall-ceiling transitions unresolved is truthful because the spec supplies only ramp height, with no run/profile, and no ceiling-transition geometry. Resolving adjacent floor, wall, corner, and ceiling faces all the way to sharp junctions nevertheless presents a complete authoritative shape that the spec cannot justify.

The derivable corner and tunnel coordinates are coherent: cuts connect `(±29.44, ±51.2)` to `(±40.96, ±39.68)`, goal openings span `x = ±8.93`, `y = 0..6.43`, and tunnels run from `z = ±51.2` to `z = ±60.0`. Centering structural trim on those limits reduces the visible opening and moves other geometry inward beyond tolerance.

The alignment test proves only that renderer-tagged vertex buffers agree with the resolver that generated them. It does not independently establish spec correctness, rendered polygon coverage, collision alignment, or conformance of untagged/frame-role boundary trim.

## Metric visible shell is disconnected from the active Rapier shell

**High severity · confirmed.** `client/src/renderer/arena.ts:13` now resolves `ARENA_GEOMETRY_SPEC` at module load. The active collider builder still declares itself the legacy shell and reads `ARENA.WIDTH`, `ARENA.LENGTH`, and `ARENA.HEIGHT` at `server/src/physics/arena.ts:38-43`; those constants remain 40 m × 60 m at `shared/src/constants/arena.ts:4-6`, versus the metric spec's 81.92 m × 102.4 m.

Cars and the ball therefore collide near `x = ±20` and `z = ±30` while visible walls sit near `x = ±40.96` and `z = ±51.2`; physical and displayed goals also differ in width, depth, and location. Do not activate the metric renderer until Task 6.1 makes production collision consume the same resolved descriptor, then add an integration assertion that both paths share one geometry identity.

## Sharp seams contradict the eight unresolved transition records

**High severity · confirmed.** The reasons recorded at `shared/src/geometry/arena-boundaries.ts:302-317` are accurate: `floorWallRamp.height = 2.56` does not determine run/profile or the planar floor endpoint, and the spec contains no wall-ceiling profile. Yet `field.floor` and `field.ceiling` cover the full octagonal footprint at `shared/src/geometry/arena-boundaries.ts:155-162`, while side, corner, and end walls span `spec.floorY..spec.ceilingHeight` at `shared/src/geometry/arena-boundaries.ts:165-235`.

The renderer selects only wall and horizontal-corner patches at `client/src/renderer/arena.ts:353-356`, so it closes every unresolved junction with a visible 90-degree seam while the test calls those surfaces “never fabricated.” Add complete transition descriptors and resolve/render all eight surfaces, or keep the adjacent finite extents unresolved and stop presenting the sharp replacements as authoritative.

## Goal and perimeter trim crosses exact limits

**High severity · confirmed.** The 0.18 m goal frame at `client/src/renderer/arena.ts:538-561` is centered at `x = ±8.93` and `y = 6.43`. Runtime `Box3` measurement shows inner post edges at `x = ±8.84` and the crossbar bottom at `y = 6.34`, a 0.09 m error; 0.11 m tunnel ribs at `client/src/renderer/arena.ts:573-584` intrude another 0.055 m from side and roof planes.

Top rails centered on boundary edges at `client/src/renderer/arena.ts:450-455` extend 0.07 m inward. Axis-aligned 0.1 m posts centered on diagonal corner planes at `client/src/renderer/arena.ts:427-440` have a `0.1 / √2 = 0.0707 m` inward projection. Offset trim so its innermost transformed point is coincident with or outside the authoritative plane, preserve goal clear bounds at frame inner edges, and include all of these meshes in tolerance checks.

## Renderer-authored metadata and a shared oracle mask bad geometry

**Medium severity · confirmed.** The renderer resolves patches at `client/src/renderer/arena.ts:13`, and the test obtains expected patches from the same function at `client/tests/stadium-camera-effects.test.ts:124`. A wrong sign, endpoint, opening, or corner interpretation is duplicated on both sides; there is no independent shared test with hard-coded v1 vertices, normals, seam connectivity, or opening topology.

The traversal admits only renderer-tagged `authoritative-face` meshes at `client/tests/stadium-camera-effects.test.ts:129-133`, excluding frame-role goal geometry and untagged ribs, posts, and rails. It then checks position-buffer coplanarity and aggregate axis extrema at `client/tests/stadium-camera-effects.test.ts:144-164`, not indices, draw range, winding, edges, area, gaps, or overlaps. A full rectangle tagged as `field.floor` would pass because it has the same plane and x/z extrema as the octagon while filling every cut corner. Add independent resolver fixtures and validate actual rendered triangle coverage with an explicit classification for every boundary-like mesh.

## Validation

`npx --no-install tsx --test client/tests/stadium-camera-effects.test.ts` passed all six tests. `npm run build -w client`, `npx tsc -b shared client --pretty false`, and `git diff HEAD --check` also passed; the findings are behavioral and coverage defects, not compilation failures.
