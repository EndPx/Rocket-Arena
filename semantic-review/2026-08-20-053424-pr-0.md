# Deterministic Wave 1 physics and authority properties

This change adds six seeded property suites around physics construction, controller behavior, grounding, finite recovery, and the server authority boundary, then hardens production boost storage so non-finite writes cannot poison projections or snapshots. The suites use 100–160 recorded-seed cases with replay checks and meaningful boundary/category variation, and the core Property 23, Property 10, and Property 19 behaviors reconstruct correctly from the implementation rather than merely from green test output. Three proof gaps remain. **Watch for:** **Medium · confirmed** — Property 11 does not independently prove its claimed steering-curve mapping; **Medium · confirmed** — Property 13 canonicalizes away the design-prescribed hit order and duplicates the production world-up fallback instead of testing the coupled zero-sum rule; **Low · confirmed** — Property 13 claims Requirement 10.10 without checking a Feature Status Record.

**Verdict**: NEEDS_CHANGES

## High-level view

All six suites meet their minimum generated-case count: Property 23 and Property 13 use 120 cases, Property 9 and Property 11 use 160, and Property 10 and Property 19 use 100. Recorded seeds, sequence regeneration, indexed replay, explicit category/boundary distributions, and no ambient randomness make failures reproducible. **Medium · confirmed:** Property 11’s curvature checks only compare fields emitted by the same controller plan, so a finite speed-independent steering constant would still satisfy the new suite despite its Requirement 8.15 claim.

Property 23 observes a real Rapier contact manifold and nonzero raw ball spin before post-step recovery while instrumenting every ball force/impulse/torque entry point. This separates collision-owned spin from scripted actuation and independently checks speed caps and world disposal.

Property 13 covers rotated Local_Down rays, every Core semantic descriptor, adjacent surfaces, observable rejection fixtures, grounded/airborne controller behavior, replay, and cleanup. **Medium · confirmed:** its trace canonicalization hides semantic-ID-first output order and its test normalizer repeats the production world-up zero-sum fallback; ordering is immaterial to an ordinary equal-weight sum, but the design couples it to the first-valid-hit fallback. **Low · confirmed:** testing one advanced-surface gate does not validate the Feature Status Record required by 10.10.

Property 10 generates 20 finite and 80 non-finite controller/tuning/body cases, traverses every numeric controller-plan field, exercises per-field last-finite body recovery, and verifies exact car/ball post-recovery caps. The closure-backed `boostAmount` retains the last finite value, clamps finite writes to `[0, 100]`, and remains compatible with kickoff capture/reset/rollback because every lifecycle helper goes through the accessor. The production-core path checks all numeric projection and snapshot fields, including timer and boost, so no false timer/inventory exposure claim remains in this scope.

Property 19 compares independently initialized Active Play worlds that differ only by forged authority extras. Matching planned controls, physics/contact state, projection, and snapshot after a meaningful fixed step prove the allow-list behavior; whole-command rejection would incorrectly suppress valid controls.

## Generated grip cases do not prove Steering_Curvature_Curve use

**Medium · confirmed.** `server/src/physics/grip-handbrake.property.test.ts:395-396` claims Requirements 8.11–8.16, but `server/src/physics/grip-handbrake.property.test.ts:573-588` only establishes that normal and handbrake plans share a base curvature, powerslide multiplies it, and signs/order are preserved. Although generated speeds include curve knots and boundaries, no assertion computes expected base curvature from configured samples; replacing the speed-dependent lookup with any finite same-sign constant leaves this property green. Add an independent piecewise-linear oracle for each generated `forwardSpeed` and `steer`, or narrow the Requirement 8.15 claim. The other suites retain non-tautological anchors: Property 9 uses inequalities and literal boundaries, Property 10 uses explicit fallback transformations and literal caps, and Property 19 isolates one changed input dimension across paired worlds.

## Rapier manifold and pre-recovery sampling own the ball spin proof

Property 23 instruments `addForce`, `addForceAtPoint`, `addTorque`, `applyImpulse`, `applyImpulseAtPoint`, and `applyTorqueImpulse` before collision (`server/src/physics/physics-construction.property.test.ts:300-359,487-488`). It reads a real contact pair and raw angular velocity immediately after `world.step()` but before recovery (`server/src/physics/physics-construction.property.test.ts:506-516`), then requires a manifold and nonzero finite raw spin (`server/src/physics/physics-construction.property.test.ts:568-584`). With zero initial ball motion and zero instrumented actuation, recovery setters cannot explain the observed spin. The generated cases also vary dimensions/damping/impact geometry, enforce caps without adding motion, and free success and synthetic-failure worlds (`server/src/physics/physics-construction.property.test.ts:288-297,674-720`).

## Grounding trace canonicalization hides the coupled order/fallback rule

**Medium · confirmed.** The design requires contact-point-index/collider-handle order and first-valid-sorted-normal fallback for a zero or invalid equal-weight sum. Production instead orders by semantic surface ID and gives zero sums world-up (`server/src/physics/grounding.ts:130-135,357-364`). The new property re-sorts output by contact-point index (`server/src/physics/grounding.property.test.ts:567-579`) and its test normalizer also defaults to world-up (`server/src/physics/grounding.property.test.ts:239-245`). Adjacent fixtures tilt both normals only 6–16 degrees around Local_Roof and intentionally produce a nonzero sum (`server/src/physics/grounding.property.test.ts:907-947`), so neither discrepancy can fail the suite.

Semantic-ID-first ordering alone does not alter a nondegenerate equal-weight sum and is not separately required by 10.1–10.5, 10.11–10.12, or 18.19. It becomes behavioral when fallback is needed: world-up may be unrelated to accepted support and outside the car-relative accepted-normal cone. Exercise aggregation with cancelling/invalid sums and assert raw order plus first-valid fallback. If Rapier makes cancellation unreachable, encode that invariant instead of preserving an untested contradictory branch.

## Advanced-surface gating is not the Feature Status Record

**Low · confirmed.** `server/src/physics/grounding.property.test.ts:1148-1150` claims Requirement 10.10, which requires every deferred Advanced surface to be identified without claiming Full Surface Driving. The suite asserts only the global gate and one `field.ceiling` rejection (`server/src/physics/grounding.property.test.ts:863-869,1158-1159`); it does not inspect a status record or the complete wall/corner/transition/ceiling set. Remove 10.10 from `Validates` or add an executable record and assert the full deferred list.

## Finite recovery closes the reachable boost projection exception

Property 10 drives malformed axes/sequences, observations, tuning, curves, timestep/index values, transforms, and velocities through recovery, then recursively checks the complete plan/exposed body state and exact cap magnitudes/directions. The production accessor cannot store non-finite values and clamps all finite writes through one closure (`server/src/rooms/rapier-room-world.ts:119-133`). Kickoff capture reads it, reset writes the finite start amount, and rollback writes the finite captured value (`server/src/rooms/rapier-room-world.ts:68-86,169-175`), so none bypass recovery. The property corrupts a reachable production car, observes last-finite recovery and both clamps in committed projections, and validates the wire snapshot (`server/src/physics/finite-bounds.property.test.ts:977-1018`). Timer handling is accurately limited to supported-lifecycle exposure and full projection/snapshot numeric traversal, not an impossible external timer write.

## Forged authority fields are omitted without suppressing controls

Property 19 reaches Active Play in both worlds, proves identical starting projections/world digests, and advances one physics step after controls alone versus controls plus forged transforms, contacts, inventory, scores, team, and phase (`server/src/rooms/server-authority.property.test.ts:572-622`). Both paths plan the same allow-listed command, non-neutral controls produce motion, and the complete world, projection, and V2 snapshot remain equal (`server/src/rooms/server-authority.property.test.ts:624-650`). Both cores are disposed in `finally` (`server/src/rooms/server-authority.property.test.ts:661-668`).

## Resource and repeatability boundaries

Rapier-backed properties free worlds on success and synthetic failure; Property 19 disposes both cores; pure controller suites allocate no external resources. No scoped suite uses wall-clock time or ambient randomness, and trace rounding supplements rather than replaces behavioral assertions.
