# AI-Generated Asset Provenance

Rocket Arena ships three original 2D brand images generated specifically for this project with ChatGPT by OpenAI. The participant directed the concepts, selected the final generations, and reviewed their spelling, transparency, composition, and intended use before inclusion. The source PNGs were downloaded into the repository and downsampled locally with high-quality bicubic resampling; they are never hotlinked at runtime.

## Primary horizontal logo

- **Generator/service:** ChatGPT by OpenAI
- **Generation date:** 2026-08-16
- **Purpose:** Primary Rocket Arena identity in the desktop lobby and at the top of the README.
- **Prompt summary:** Create an original horizontal `ROCKET ARENA` wordmark with a mechanical energy-ball emblem, blue/orange motion trails, bold competitive lettering, and a transparent background.
- **Participant direction and selection:** The participant directed the horizontal lockup and blue/orange team palette, selected this result, and approved the final spelling and transparent treatment. The shipped file was resized only; its composition was not redrawn.
- **Source dimensions:** 1981×793 PNG
- **Shipped dimensions:** 1024×410 PNG with alpha
- **Local path:** `client/src/assets/generated/rocket-arena-logo.png`

## Originality and gameplay boundary

No third-party franchise files, logos, geometries, textures, characters, vehicle designs, ball-panel layouts, stadium designs, or 3D models were imported into these assets or the repository. These images are presentation-only brand art: they do not define gameplay dimensions, collision geometry, physics state, or hitboxes. All gameplay geometry remains procedural and source-controlled.
